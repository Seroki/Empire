Empire

Empire is a tick-based strategy game inspired by the original Evony: Age I PC experience, rebuilt for a modern web-based platform.

At its core, Empire embraces the unforgiving gameplay that made classic browser strategy games addictive:

Build and manage thriving cities.
Research powerful technologies through expanded and modernized tech trees.
Recruit, train, and equip legendary heroes.
Capture enemy cities and steal valuable heroes.
Visit Inns to recruit new talent or purchase loyalty to retain your best commanders.
Fight famine, resource shortages, and rival empires.
Risk losing everything if you make the wrong move.

The game preserves the brutal competitive nature of the original experience where your strongest hero can be captured, your cities can starve, and years of planning can be undone by a stronger opponent.

Beyond the Original

While Empire remains faithful to the classic gameplay loop, it introduces new mechanics and expanded progression systems designed to keep the late game engaging long after traditional city development reaches its limit.

The Holy Cities

Players may only develop their own cities to Level 10. This cap keeps the core gameplay balanced and ensures that expansion, strategy, and conquest remain the primary paths to power.

But beyond the safety of your empire lies something far greater.

Scattered throughout the wilderness are legendary Level 12 NPC Holy Cities.

These ancient strongholds cannot be built. They cannot be purchased. They can only be conquered.

A Holy City grants unique capabilities unavailable anywhere else in the game, providing advantages that ordinary empires can never obtain. Owning one marks you as a true ruler of the realm and a target for every ambitious king, queen, and warlord on the server.

The question is simple:

Will your empire be strong enough to claim one?

Or will you become another ruler whose armies vanished before the gates of a Holy City?

Welcome to Empire. Build. Conquer. Survive. Rule.
