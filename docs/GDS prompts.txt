One More Volume I'd Add

For your project specifically, I'd add a dedicated "Historical Reference & Recreation Notes" volume.

For every mechanic, classify it as:

Confirmed — Verified from archived game data, screenshots, or multiple independent sources.
Strongly Inferred — Consistent with observed gameplay but not directly documented.
Custom — An intentional enhancement for your remake.

For example:

Building: Academy

Original Behavior:
Confirmed

Research Queue:
Confirmed

Research Speed Formula:
Strongly Inferred

Additional Research Categories:
Custom

This gives you a clear boundary between faithfully recreating Evony Age I and extending it with your own ideas. Years from now, you'll still know which mechanics came from the original game and which were design decisions you made.
